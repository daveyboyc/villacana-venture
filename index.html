How this prototype is made

A walk built from the real map

The coastline, Playa del Sol, the named lanes, the swimming pools, and the houses volunteers have traced all come from OpenStreetMap. Streets that do not have a house drawn yet get a row of ordinary white terraces, which is what this urbanization actually looks like, so the walk is a neighbourhood rather than an empty plan.

The two beach pictures are Creative Commons photographs of the Estepona coast, the same shore Villacana faces. The empty frames are yours. Put a picture in public/photos and point album.json at it.

You move the way you would in a small 3D platformer: walk, run, jump, and a camera over your shoulder. It is an original stroll, not a Nintendo game.
