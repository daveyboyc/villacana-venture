      Repeat the pack, one physical scene at a time

      1. Inspect full sources. Group by signs, architecture and observed connections. Keep camera GPS separate.
      2. Inventory first. Openings, ceiling, walls, levels and route connections precede finishes and planting.
      3. Draw visible evidence. Number locators and traces; retain occluded edges and uncertain identity explicitly.
      4. Compare with the model. Link a pinned render/feature, or record absent, partial, material-only or unassessed.
      5. Resolve placement, then build. Use the existing measured review gate. Recheck every component in the after render.
