# South pool reference pass

Built 2026-10-12. Walk it at `slice.html?pool=south`; Overview shows the layout.

Sources: the registry (pool and house outlines), the aerial photo
(`public/map/ground.jpg`), Dave's photo `IMG_2188.jpg`, and three web photos Dave sent
(`south-pool-01`, `-02`, `-03`), which are kept in the private reference repo and are
not bundled in the public site. Apple imagery was compared by eye only.

## Confirmed

- **Pool outline and position:** the registry outline, unchanged. It agrees with the
  aerial: a three-lobed clover with a short stub on the north-east. No missing lobe.
- **Stone deck** right round the pool, about 2.4 m wide (aerial and all pool photos).
- **Lawn** between the two terraces from the cross path down to the seaward wall, and a
  smaller upper lawn north of the cross path (aerial).
- **Seaward wall and fence:** a sandstone retaining wall with a dark mesh fence
  along the seaward edge (`IMG_2349`, corroborated from the lawn in `IMG_2188`).
  The earlier white-wall reading from distant pool-side views is superseded by
  that close paseo view. It is one unbroken boundary with no gate; the player
  cannot walk through it. Short legacy end returns remain approximate.
- **Pool fittings**, each placed from two or three photos against the surveyed outline:
  steps on the north-east stub, two ladders, the white pump box, three life rings.
- **The large palm** north-west of the lawn by the cross path (web photo 01 and a clear
  crown on the aerial).
- **Clipped hedge** in front of the west terrace's patios (`IMG_2188`, web photo 02).
- **No glass fence** round the pool, by Dave's earlier decision.

## Guessed or approximate

- **Lawn, path and wall lines** are traced by hand from the aerial and are good to
  about 2 m (`data/south_pool.json`).
- **Low curved white walls on the lawn:** the photos show them on the west, south-west,
  south and north-west sides of the pool. Only the west run and the one edging the
  upper lawn are drawn, where the aerial shows a pale line. Their exact curves and the
  gap in the west run are guesses. Hedges on them follow the same rule as the other
  courtyards.
- **Path from the deck to the west terrace:** the photos show crazy paving leaving the
  deck; its line is a guess.
- **Ends of the seaward wall:** the short return at each end, joining the wall to a
  house so there is no way round, is a guess. At the west end the wall is drawn up to
  2 m inland of where the aerial shows it, to stay clear of the paseo line, which comes
  from OpenStreetMap and sits slightly too far inland there.
- **Hedge distance from the house fronts** (about 3 m) and the pump box, which the
  reviewer thinks is about 1 m further north-west.
- **House fronts** (balconies, shutters, porches) are generated by rule, as elsewhere.

## Left out

- **The tall palm by the seaward wall** in `IMG_2188`. It is real, but the two readings
  of its position differ by about 4 m, so the reviewer rejected it. The readings are
  about (−89.3, 210.6) and (−87.5, 214) in map metres.
- A parasol and a sun lounger: movable, and not placeable to 3 m.
- Things with no kind in `data/kinds.json`: short green bollard lights on the lawn, two
  shower posts by the deck, pool-rules boards, a lamp column by the fence.
- A candelabra cactus and bougainvillea by the east terrace: seen, but not placeable.

## Possible error in the registry

The single-storey pavilion at the seaward end of the west terrace appears on the
aerial as a white block just outside the registry outline, near (−89, 214). It is not
drawn. The west end of the seaward wall stands roughly where its east wall would be.

## How it was made

The handover method: ground traced in `scripts/build_skeleton.py` from
`data/south_pool.json`; a patch pack; one agent proposed 23 objects and a second,
separate agent reviewed them (20 accepted, 3 rejected; `proposals/south-pool.json`
keeps every verdict and reason). The north, middle and main pool layouts and the
seafront were not changed.

## Seaward level and wall reconciliation — issue #37

IMG_2349 was inspected at full size alongside IMG_2188, the south-pool contact
sheet and the map aerial. From the paseo, the sandstone face supports the lawn;
the green mesh stands at lawn level, with the pool deck behind it. The existing
paseo-wall pass already supplies the approximate 1 m difference by lowering the
paseo to -1 m while the south pool and both lawns stay at datum 0. No additional
platform or slope is supported by these views, so those heights are preserved.
The Dalí rise, restaurant-to-main-pool drop and sea profile are unchanged.

The old `skeleton.seaWall` trace records this same boundary, not an additional
white wall above the sandstone. Shared stretches now disappear consistently from
the legacy wall mesh, map overlay and collision. The sandstone wall supplies all
three there; the short old white returns still join the houses. Their extent
remains approximate and unphotographed, as recorded above. No route, registry
footprint or coastline is retraced in this task.

`data/south_pool.json:seawardLevel` records the photo evidence and height limits.
`check_south_pool_level.py` checks the existing flat lawn/pool datum and paseo
difference; the layout browser check builds both wall meshes and verifies shared
segments, collision, lawn datum and coping, then walks the paseo both ways.
Camera comparisons use IMG_2349 at its existing pin/heading in Details mode.
They are approximate third-person views, not calibrated image matches.
