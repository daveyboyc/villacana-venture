# Cervantes available-source inventory

This records the inspected evidence for Calle Miguel de Cervantes, before further
path or wall reconstruction. Open the [comparison viewer](https://daveyboyc.github.io/villacana-venture/path-network/cervantes/index.html)
or [select Cervantes in the canonical map inspector](https://daveyboyc.github.io/villacana-venture/?view=map&network=1&feature=path-coverage:cervantes).

## What is accounted for

- 51 distinct full sources: 26 area-tagged public photos, four adjoining public
  views, the Esri ground frame, eight private map/3D views and 12 private pool photos.
  35 support observations; 16 are inspected but unsuitable for this lane/portal
  identification scope. Three exact Drive storage mirrors add no independent evidence.
- 30 component rows, including finite path margins, ground walls versus upper
  facades/roof outlines, the Dalí apron, drains, garden door, steps, patio fragments,
  and the signed CALLE MIGUEL interior.
- 41 source-pixel annotations (17 finite traces and 24 locators) and separate schematic [plan](plan.svg) and
  [sections](tunnel-section.svg). These express visible relationships, not scale
  or map registration. Dashed locators identify components; they are not edge traces.
- Per-component [model assessments](model-comparisons.json) and six actual game
  captures pinned to `83ca94df5f0cdab85e5972a8ac8c8cce69daced9`. Cameras are
  uncalibrated context. Missing visibility and unresolved identity are reported as
  not comparable, rather than treated as a photographic match.

The shared Elena junction has three route treads beside a smooth ramp. The private
brown doorway has two projecting treads plus a raised sill. They are different
structures. The Miguel photo has a flat covered ceiling and far arch, two near
wooden door sets on opposite sides, farther barred openings, patterned paving,
pebble inset, bench and lantern. The camera-side portal is outside the photo.
Neither world portal nor its host is identified. Silvia/Jorge/Elena and legacy
passage candidates remain separate; a street plaque alone does not join them.

## What still needs work

Five evidence requests remain: registered ground edges, Miguel identity/portals,
concealed patio joins, shared-junction measured levels and the far eastern terminal.
All source-to-world transforms and component map geometry remain null. Private
pixels are excluded from public outputs; their source hashes and limitations are
recorded. No new 3D geometry, collision, physical access or placement approval is
claimed by this inventory.
