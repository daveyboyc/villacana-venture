# North pool to Pintor Dalí

PR #292 placed a provisional 44 m tiled path and two entrance kerbs between the north pool and Dalí. Review traced all six source photos to the west end of Calle Eva at the **middle pool**. The model already places that lane and its kerbs in `data/eva_lane.json`. Its Dalí entrance lies about 88 m from the proposed north-pool entrance.

The Calle Eva [lane review](../../../refs/issues/eva-lane-details.md) identifies IMG_2551–2553. The [8 November photo inventory](../../PHOTOS_2026-11-08.md) follows IMG_2705–2712 from Dalí to the middle pool’s south-west approach.

The unsupported north-pool path and kerbs were withdrawn. Ground-level coverage of the north-pool approach is still needed to establish whether there is a through path. The field request `north-pool-dali-approach` asks for overlapping views with identifiable house and patio corners.
