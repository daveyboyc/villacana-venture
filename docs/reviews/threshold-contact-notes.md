# Threshold ground contact follow-up

The merged issue #57 geometry remains the source of truth: two red sills west of Elena's lawns, the 25.95 m Dalí frontage with its lawn ending and kerb turning inward, and the south approach aligned to the registry recess with a right-side flare. Heights and placement remain estimates documented in `data/door_sills.json`. The photographed doors and the passage behind the south arch remain unmodelled; no registry footprint or opening changes.

The previous movement sampler added the feature rise to the inherited 0.06 m player datum, while the visible sills stood on 0.025 m paving and the Dalí features stood on 0.272 m asphalt. Its browser checks accepted those absolute differences. The local correction now matches the visible datum, including the lawn's 0.01 m depression, kerb's 0.012 m cap and flush drain surface. The first metre around the shared feature rings retains the corrected datum, with a two metre blend to inherited player ground; this blend is a movement approximation rather than surveyed terrain. Solid house and pool interiors are excluded, and remote inherited mesh/player differences remain outside this change.

The existing road ribbon stopped a few centimetres short of the lawn-side kerb and 1.85 m short of the path kerb after the lawn ended. A finite asphalt apron joins those unchanged edges, only from the existing full-height kerb start to the frontage end. Its L-shaped ring is shared by the map, rendered surfaces and movement correction. The source photos show asphalt directly at the kerb; the exact apron dimensions remain estimates. The road axis, bays and house footprint remain unchanged.

The approach already had a one metre level top reaching the solid facade, so its geometry is preserved. The browser regression now checks actual settled shoe soles against surface raycasts, both sides of the old asphalt/apron contact, the approach toe, side kerbs and landing at the collision stop, in addition to the existing walking routes and source checks. Independent photo comparison and the final diagnostic images are recorded with the review artifacts; runtime validation is reported in the PR.
