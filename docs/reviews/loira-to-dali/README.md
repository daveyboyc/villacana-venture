# Calle Loira to Pintor Dalí

[Esri aerial trace](aerial.jpg) in the existing east/south metre frame. White
lines show the mapped road centres, cyan shows registry building outlines,
magenta shows the wall, orange shows the Juan Gris paving and green shows
the mapped verge. The existing road and registry footprints guide the tie-in.
The Apple Maps north-pool west view (reference 08) corroborates the narrow
boundary band, grass verge and bend.

The extension continues the white wall and adjoining paving from the previous
model endpoint to the modelled Dalí road edge. The last few metres are fitted
to the existing mapped carriageway. The aerial shows a continuous band, with
vegetation obscuring the wall foot. Inferred dimensions and materials carry
over from the adjacent photographed stretch. The existing arched opening
stays at its original station. The reddish patch near the old end is ambiguous
in the aerial. Its edges, enclosure and any gates remain covered by the
`loira-outside-wall` photo request.

The model follows the checked-in LiDAR terrain. Existing survey data gives no
wall-top levels. The new wall is a continuous estimate and the trace has no
solved ground-level photos or independent measured residuals.

The independent plan check found no overlap with the nominal Loira or Dalí
carriageways or the registry buildings. The paved end lies about 0.6–1.1 m
from the Dalí asphalt edge, leaving a short verge where the threshold is
unclear. [Previous Inspect view](inspect-before.jpg) and
[updated Layout](inspect-layout.jpg) and [Show details](inspect-details.jpg)
show the change at the bend.
