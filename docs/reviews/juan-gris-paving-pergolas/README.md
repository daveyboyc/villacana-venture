# Calle de Juan Gris paving and pergolas

IMG_3136, IMG_3146 and IMG_3152 show four lane-aligned square terracotta courses.
The tiles use the shared square-tile swatch, with narrow grey mortar margins and
a pitch of about 0.29 m within the modelled 1.28 m strip. The grid follows the
traced path through its bends. The pitch is a model dimension and the
appearance toward Dalí is inferred from the adjoining photographed lane.

The model reuses the Elena beam builder. The wall and paving axes are kept,
with nominal beam headroom of 2.12 m above the modelled lane and narrow
house-side supports outside the paved strip. The survey supplies the ground
datum. The stations, spans, beam count where obscured and support dimensions
are estimates from unsolved cameras. The western span is the least certain.

[Inspect Layout](inspect-layout.jpg) and [Show Details](inspect-details.jpg) show the lane-aligned joints and open
spans. The independent plan check found the pergola posts clear of the paved
strip and the pergola footprints clear of the mapped roads and registry
buildings. Browser checks confirmed four tile courses and four spans.
