## Conventions and traps

**Frames.** Map metres are +x east and +z south.

- *Car park to the hotel's low block.* Positions are often given in the long wall's frame:
  - origin (-35.7, 23.9), the wall's south end;
  - s along (0.5191, 0.8547), south-east;
  - e = east of the wall's centreline, the road side.

  `data/picasso_service_frontage.json` `frame` holds it.
- *Heights.* Survey heights are over the datum: the middle pool deck, 5.00 m in the survey and model zero. Road and
  car-park paving is drawn 0.27 m above the terrain, so a surveyed top is drawn at `top + 0.27`. That is the `PAVING`
  constant in the Picasso modules.

**Laser survey.**
- Fetch the tiles once with `python3 scripts/lidar_check.py fetch`. They need `pip install rasterio pyproj matplotlib`.
- Read heights with `python3 scripts/lidar_grid.py X0 X1 Z0 Z1 [step] --surface [--frame=ox,oz,ax,az]`.
- The surface model is a 2 m grid, so edges are good to about 1 m.
- **A spike can be a tree.** The tower was first put on a palm at (-6.5, 73.5); check every spike on the aerial and
  against the photos' bearings.
- **Terrain.** `data/terrain.json` is generated by `scripts/lidar_check.py grid`. Do not hand-edit it.

**Inspect renders.** Use `scripts/shoot_inspect.cjs`.
- *Camera at a photo's pin.* For heading h (compass degrees) and distance d, use target = pin + d·(sin h, −cos h) and
  bearing = 360 − h. Pitch asin(1.7/d) gives eye height.
- *Time.* Each view takes 3-5 minutes, so run batches in the background. Set
  `PLAYWRIGHT_CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium` and run `npm ci` first.
- **`scripts/shoot_views.cjs`** renders from the photo again (package 1, fixed 26 Nov 2026: it now asks for
  `view=walk`). `--pose` was never affected.

**Ground plan.** `check_ground_plan.py` refuses:
- a surface inside a standing house;
- an unmarked overlap (use `above`);
- a surface without a confidence entry.

A concrete surface raised to almost the street ribbon's height (0.27 m) breaks through the asphalt where the terrain
bends, so keep fills at their default lift or well clear of it.

**Streets.** A street is one width. Edit `data/street_layout.json` **and** both `streets` and
`boundaryReference.roads` in `public/map/skeleton.json`. The skeleton is patched in place as one compact JSON line, so
round-trip it with `separators=(',', ':')`. Allowed surfaces are `asphalt` and `terracotta`.

**Hand-formatted JSON.** `data/build_queue.json`, `data/confidence.json`, `data/photo_checks.json`,
`data/reference_tags.json` and `data/evidence_requests.json` each have their own indentation. Insert blocks as text
and confirm the file still parses. Do not re-dump the whole file.

**Merging.** This branch's earlier PRs were squash-merged, and force-push is refused here. Start each new piece of
work from `origin/main` (`git checkout -B <branch> origin/main`). If the remote branch still holds old history:
1. run `git fetch origin <branch> && git merge -X ours FETCH_HEAD`;
2. regenerate with `python3 scripts/assign_photo_map.py && python3 scripts/build_references.py --sheets` (the merge
   duplicates rows in generated `refs/` files);
3. push normally.

**Red checks that are not ours.** These fail on `main` too: `check_seafront`, `check_seafront_fence`,
`test_layout_browser` (stops at the coastal-gate button, `:795`), `test_overview_browser` (`:45`) and
`test_photo_map_browser` (the "Barraka beach boat" search also returns IMG_3051), which is the CI `validate` job. The
placement gate stays red without solved cameras. Merge anyway and say so in one PR comment (AGENTS.md, Merging).

**Review.** Every structure stage needs a reviewer subagent that did not make the change. Give it:
- the full-size photos;
- the renders;
- the aerial;
- the data.

It caught real errors every time in this session: the gate on the wrong wall, the "green-door building" that was the
bin store's own bay, a missing gate and footway, and the tower on a palm.
