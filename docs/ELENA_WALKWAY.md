## Walking

Nothing here is solid. The player stands on the visible floor (`elenaWalkwayGroundOffset` is added to the walk
ground like the other step flights, risers 0.133 m against the 0.45 m ground step), so the walkway is still
open end to end; the only change to Walk is that the ground rises with the steps/ramp by 0.40 m.

## Not built / open

- The arched single brown door with barred grille and brass knob (IMG_2692, IMG_2813): which wall, where.
- Recessed door bays, louvred windows, the stepped piers and imposts at both arches, the second pebble mat
  (IMG_2547 shows one at another passage), the west end beyond the arch (private 36/34 gates already built).
- Photographed dimensions are not surveyed. The camera pins of these photos are about +/-6 m and the model
  frame comes from the registry strip, which sits about 1-1.5 m off the laser survey. The renders are hand-set
  cameras (unsolved starting poses), so they resemble the photographs but do not line up pixel for pixel.
- IMG_2541-2547 show the separate Cervantes to Eva inner walk-through (passage `likely`). Its floor, doors and
  lantern are the same kit; only the Elena walkway is built here.
- The Elena/Cervantes junction details of IMG_2540/2511 (two grey stone inset slabs in the Cervantes paving, the
  CALLE ELENA tile sign on the lawn kerb wall) are not built.

No placement approval is claimed: the footprint is unchanged, the added floor/steps stand inside it, and no
independent reviewer has compared them with the photographs.
