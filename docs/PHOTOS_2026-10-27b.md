## Findings by area

- **Beach gateway (main pool / paseo).** IMG_2602 shows white piers with an arched coping over the
  iron gate, and green rigid mesh continuing from the pier towards Barraka. A terracotta landing
  meets the pink lawn path. IMG_2485 shows the COMUNIDAD VILLACANA ZONA PRIVADA plaque, a
  CCTV sign and plumbago on the outer pier.
- **East of Barraka.** IMG_2623–2627 show, behind a chain-link fence on a sandstone wall, a
  disused flat-roofed glazed pavilion and a white single-storey house with a green dado. The
  paseo continues east between that fence and the sand (IMG_2621/2622/2628). Barraka's beach
  side is a white two-storey block with a brown band (IMG_2618).
- **Seaward lanes.** Covered passages repeat one pattern:
  - an arch, a blue-inset tiled floor, a pebble mat and lanterns;
  - at a level change, a red ribbed ramp beside two or three tiled steps (IMG_2569/2570,
    2574, 2581/2582, 2592, 2656/2657, and 2669/2670 under CALLE SILVIA 2–20).

  Several lanes end at arched iron gates onto the seafront lawns (IMG_2576/2577, 2646/2647,
  2650). Each one still needs placing before anything is built from it.
- **Cervantes.** IMG_2541 shows the CALLE MIGUEL plaque in a covered walkway with an arch at its
  far end. IMG_2513 shows the curved-coping garden door at the Elena junction from the lane.
- **Elena.** IMG_2517/2689 show the CALLE ELENA 18–36 ramp and steps again. IMG_2683–2685 show a
  topiary garden in front of a house with timber balconies.
- **Middle and south pools.** IMG_2678/2679 show a gate from the Eva side into the middle pool
  garden. IMG_2641/2642 show a covered passage ending at an arched iron gate onto crazy paving
  that leads to the south pool.
