## Useful modeling evidence

- IMG_2273–2275: Cervantes’ straight narrow route, weathered square terracotta, window grilles and lantern brackets; a drain and sloping exit onto Dalí. Do not infer a measured ramp rise from these photos.
- IMG_2276–2279: Dalí’s junction/bay markings, house-side footpath, traditional lanterns, outer chain-link screening, low white curb and mature trees.
- IMG_2280: an irregular flagstone pedestrian entrance, distinct from Cervantes’ square tiles. Its GPS anchor is also close to the mapped Calle Jorge connection; a street sign is not readable here, so the entrance is not relabeled with a new street name.
- IMG_2281–2284: the seaward turning area, wall and rigid metal mesh, modern flat-headed lamp, yellow curb/no-parking markings and a narrow pedestrian gate. The gate is specific evidence of a pedestrian opening; do not extend an uninterrupted solid fence across it without a deliberate game-boundary decision.
- IMG_2283: raised stone approach with several shallow steps, flared white curbs and an archway below shuttered windows and a barrel-tile canopy. This is separate from both the Cervantes ramp and the big-pool access.
