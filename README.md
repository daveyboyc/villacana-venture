# Villacana reference images

Private reference material for the villacana-venture game. Not for publishing:
these are screenshots of Apple Maps and Google Maps, used only to compare against by eye.

**Agents: start from `refs/README.md` in villacana-venture**, not from this file. Every
image here is catalogued there by area, with what it shows, its viewpoint and what it
is useful for. `contact-sheets/<area>.jpg` holds numbered thumbnails of every reference
for one area, in the same order as that area's brief. They are generated by
`scripts/build_references.py --sheets` in villacana-venture; do not edit them by hand.
New images added here need an entry in villacana-venture's `data/reference_tags.json`.

Facts confirmed by Dave (2026-10-11):
- Villacana has three small clover-shaped pools and one large main pool (with a round toddler pool beside it).
- Buildings are white, with brown wooden Persian-blind shutters.

Decisions from Dave (2026-10-11):
- Skip the glass safety fence round the pools.
- Eventually all plants and small details should be textures and sprites, not 3D shapes.
- The player should be able to get into the pools.

What photos/03 to 07 show of the north pool:
- Pool: a cream stone deck two to three metres wide following the pool's curves, steps
  into the water at one lobe, steel ladders, life rings on posts.
- Ground: lawn runs right up to the deck. The path is grey-brown crazy-paving stone and
  runs round the outside edge, along the foot of the garden walls.
- Garden walls: white, about a metre high, curving, some holding back raised patios
  with tiled steps up. Hedges grow behind and over them. Small arched garden gates with
  brown wooden doors.
- Houses: flat roofs with parapets, water tanks, solar panels. White moulded surrounds
  above windows. Two kinds of balcony: wooden ones with posts and a little clay-tile
  roof, and plain black iron ones. Awnings over ground-floor patios. Patio furniture.
- One building on the south side has an old barrel clay-tile roof, mottled with lichen.
- Planting: bird-of-paradise by the pool, a yucca, small feathery trees, a pollarded
  tree, magenta bougainvillea on walls, hibiscus hedges with pink and red flowers.
- Backdrop looking north: tall dark pines and a monkey-puzzle tree, palms, then blue
  mountains with cloud on the peaks.

What photos/02 shows: a frosted-band glass safety fence right round the pool deck; a
steel pool ladder; pale cream stone deck; a grey-brown crazy-paving stone path; lawn
patches and clipped hedges; a large bird-of-paradise plant and a feathery-leaved tree; a
two-storey terrace with a pitched clay-tile roof and white chimneys with small tiled
caps; arched brown shutters; a wooden-posted balcony; a low white wall along the path;
the sea visible beyond the roofs.

What photos/01 shows: thick white rendered walls with soft rounded edges; brown louvred
shutters with arched tops, door height on both floors; black wrought-iron balcony rails
and window grilles; low white garden walls with curved ends edging the lawn; a
terracotta-tiled path; clipped round hedges; a tall candelabra cactus; climbers on the
walls; a short green bollard light; chimneys and water tanks on the flat roofs; deep
green lawn; blue sky with big white cumulus.

## Drive photos (2026-10-12)

`photos/drive/` holds web-sized copies of the 67 photos Dave uploaded to his Google
Drive folder "Villacana" (IMG_2182 to IMG_2256, taken 11 and 12 October 2026), with
embedded data removed. `photos/drive/manifest.json` gives each one's position in map
metres and compass heading, read from the phone's location data. Phone positions are
good to roughly 5 to 10 m and worse under archways. The full-size originals stay in
Drive. Rebuild with `scripts/index_photos.py` in the villacana-venture repo.

Useful facts in them: IMG_2254 shows the sign "CALLE ELENA 18"; IMG_2187 and 2191 show
house-number signs in the arched passages; lanes are terracotta tile throughout; arched
covered passages run through the terraces; white tiled benches stand on the lawns.
